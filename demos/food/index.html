Recipe recommender

Content-based similarity over recipes. Free-text ingredient lines are parsed down to ingredient tokens — quantities, units and prep words stripped — then combined with dish tags and a coarse nutrition band into a TF-IDF profile per recipe.

Why content-based, and not collaborative

This corpus has no user ratings at all — so collaborative filtering is impossible on it, and claiming a hybrid model would be fiction. The honest design is content-based similarity, which also means it handles a brand-new recipe with zero interaction history.

Evaluated by tag agreement: 96% of the top-5 neighbours share a dish tag with the query recipe, against 38.5% for random pairs — a 2.5× lift. The parsing is where the real work is: 2/3 cup panko has to become panko, or every recipe looks similar because they all contain cups and teaspoons.
