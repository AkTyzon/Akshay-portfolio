Movie recommender

Item-item collaborative filtering over the MovieLens ratings matrix. Pick any film and it returns the closest neighbours by cosine similarity on mean-centred ratings — no genre matching, only what people actually watched together.

How it was evaluated

A recommender that looks plausible is easy; one that beats simply showing the most popular films is not. Each user's most recent highly-rated film is hidden, the rest of their history is used to score candidates, and I check whether the hidden film comes back in the top 10.

The model hits 15.5% against a popularity baseline's 5.9% on the same protocol — a 2.6× lift. Similarities are precomputed offline and shipped as static JSON, so this page needs no server and answers instantly.
